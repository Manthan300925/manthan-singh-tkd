I'm Manthan Singh, an Indian Taekwondo athlete whose journey has been shaped by consistent training, competitive experience and the guidance of dedicated coaches.

From KVS regional competitions to representing the KVS Chandigarh Region at national meets, every experience has strengthened my commitment to learning, discipline and growth.

For me, Taekwondo is more than a sport. It is a practice of focus, respect, resilience and continuous improvement.
